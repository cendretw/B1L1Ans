篇章文章閱讀 (Remote Learning)

9% 配分

During the COVID-19 pandemic*, remote learning became a new way of receiving education. Since then, it has changed many people's learning experiences. In fact, remote learning comes with both benefits and challenges. (第31題主旨段落)

Remote learning brings people some noticeable benefits. A major advantage lies in its flexibility* and comfort. Imagine this: A student gets up in the morning, enjoys a relaxing breakfast, and prepares for a day of learning right in his or her own home. There is no need to rush to school or follow the dress code. This allows students to learn in an environment that they find the most comfortable. (第32題答案句) Having a learning space that is just right for students makes the learning better. Also, having family members help with learning can create a team-like and fun environment, making the learning even better.

On the other hand, remote learning presents certain challenges. Technical problems can interrupt learning, even though teachers are available to help. Also, looking at screens for too long can cause discomfort and tired eyes. Therefore, students should take regular breaks and do some quick physical* activities to stay focused and energetic. Another challenge includes reduced face-to-face social interaction*, which is very important for learning how to talk to others and understand their feelings. Balancing screen time with real-life activities is very important for a person's overall happiness and health.

In conclusion, while remote learning offers notable advantages in terms of flexibility and comfort, it also presents challenges that require careful consideration. With a positive attitude and active steps, students can handle these challenges and make the most of this special educational opportunity. (第33題答案句: 積極正向態度)
